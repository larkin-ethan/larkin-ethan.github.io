About Me

I got into programming the unglamorous way — fixing a spreadsheet macro for a summer job that nobody else wanted to touch, and realizing I liked untangling the broken thing more than I liked the job itself. Two years later, that's still basically what I do, just with better tools.

Outside of work I'm usually deep in a board game rulebook, attempting a new bread recipe that will probably not rise properly, or losing an argument with my fig tree about whether it's getting enough light. I read more nonfiction than I finish, and I have strong, mostly indefensible opinions about tabs vs. spaces that I will not be sharing here.

At work, I care about the same thing in both code and conversation: say the true thing plainly. I'd rather ship something small and honest about its limitations than something impressive that quietly doesn't work.
